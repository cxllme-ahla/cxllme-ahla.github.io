Ini postingan pertama hari ini.


Oke — kamu mau prompt untuk desain karakter: karakter utama di tengah, lalu di sisi-sisinya ada potongan (close-up) aksesoris & detail pakaian yang terpisah.

Berikut contoh prompt (bahasa Inggris — biasanya hasilnya lebih konsisten):


---

Prompt contoh (style “character sheet”):

> Character design sheet of a main character standing in the center, full body, neutral pose, clear lighting. Around the character, show separated clothing parts and accessories (close-up panels) such as shoes, belt, fabric texture, jewelry, buttons, and outfit layers. Clean layout, white background, thin lines around the detail boxes, labeled design notes, professional artbook style, high detail, soft shadows, 4k, highly detailed illustration.




---

Kalau mau versi lebih cute/anime:

> Anime character design sheet, main character full body in the center, natural pose, soft colors. Around the character, show zoomed clothing details and accessories in separate boxes (hair clip, shoes, jacket folds, fabric texture, ribbons). Organized layout like a character reference sheet, simple white background, neat labels, clean lineart, high resolution.




---

Kalau mau versi lebih realistis:

> Realistic character concept sheet, full-body character in the middle, cinematic lighting. Surrounding panels show isolated clothing parts and accessories: boots, gloves, necklace, fabric texture, stitching, buckles. Professional concept art style, minimal background, designer notes, high resolution, ultra detailed.




---

Tips supaya hasil makin bagus

Tambahkan:

✔ jenis karakter
✔ gender / umur
✔ tema (fantasy, modern, school, cyberpunk, dll)
✔ mood warna
✔ medium (watercolor, digital painting, anime style, dll)

Contoh:

> teenage girl, modern casual outfit, pastel colors, digital painting style



Kalau kamu mau, tulis:

1️⃣ gaya (anime / realistis / semi-realistic, dll)
2️⃣ tema karakter
3️⃣ detail pakaian/aksesoris yang ingin ditampilkan

Nanti aku bantu bikin prompt yang lebih pas 👍
